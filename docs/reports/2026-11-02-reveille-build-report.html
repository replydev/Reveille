What was built

AreaWhat it does
Scheduling engine (:engine)Pure Kotlin. Policies of weekly rules (every N weeks, start/end dates, pauses, per-day skip), one-off alarms that replace or add to a day, correct daylight-saving handling, and a planner that rings or reports every overdue alarm.
StorageRoom and DataStore in device-protected storage, so alarms work after a reboot before you unlock. The alarm log is the single record of each alarm and is pruned after 60 days.
Alarm firingsetAlarmClock main alarm plus a backup 2 minutes later. Ringing runs as a foreground service with a full-screen screen over the lock screen. A "ring guard" alarm re-rings until you dismiss or snooze. A fallback notification rings by itself if the service can't start. The bundled sound is used before unlock.
RecoveryRe-arms on boot (before unlock too), app update, time and time zone change, app open, widget update and a 6-hour watchdog. Alarms up to 30 minutes late are rung; older ones raise a "Missed alarm" notification. An alarm ringing during a force-stop is recovered when the app is next opened.
Reliability screenChecks notifications, full-screen permission, alarm channels, exact alarms, battery optimization and alarm volume. HyperOS steps (Autostart, No restrictions, lock-screen and pop-up permissions, lock in Recents). Test alarm and alarm log.
ScreensHome 14-day timeline (skip, unskip, undo), policy list with status and editor, one-off editor with quick date chips, Settings.
WidgetNext alarm with a Skip that only acts on the alarm it shows; warns when reliability checks fail.

Known limits (by design)

  • Force-stop deletes all alarms until Reveille is opened again. Tapping the widget counts; restarting the phone does not.
  • Skipping a policy alarm skips that policy for the whole day.
  • While an alarm is unresolved, the ring guard's time shows as the "next alarm" in the status bar.
